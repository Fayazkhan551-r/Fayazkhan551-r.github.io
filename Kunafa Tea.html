Welcome to Kunafa Tea

A Taste of Damascus in Bradford

Located inside Ultimate Fireworks on Thornton Road, Kunafa Tea is your local gateway to the rich, vibrant flavors of the Middle East. We specialize in authentic Damascene food, crafting everything from comforting small plates to satisfying mains.

Beyond our savoury dishes like grilled kibbeh and fresh tabbouleh, we are renowned for our traditional desserts. Whether you are craving the sweet, warm cheese of our signature Kunafa or the rich crunch of creamy baklava, our airy and casual environment is the perfect place to enjoy it.
