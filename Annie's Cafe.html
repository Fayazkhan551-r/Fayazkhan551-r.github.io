Welcome to Annie's

Your Friendly Local Spot for Top Quality Cafe Food

At Annie’s Cafe, we believe in doing the simple things exceptionally well. We're a local favourite in Bloxwich known for serving up properly cooked, hot, and tasty food with a smile.

Whether you're stopping by for our famous hot breakfast, grabbing a quick freshly-made sandwich on your lunch break, or using our convenient curbside pickup, we guarantee fresh ingredients daily. We proudly source quality meat from local butchers to ensure every bite is spot on.
